# ADDIE

## Core idea

Analysis: Identifying the target audience, learning goals, constraints, and specific training needs.
Design: Mapping out the learning objectives, lesson plans, assessments, media selection, and overall instructional strategy.
Development: Creating and assembling the actual content, materials, interactive elements, and coursework.
Implementation: Deploying the course, delivering the training, and introducing the learning materials to the audience.
Evaluation: Assessing the effectiveness of the training, gathering learner feedback, and measuring outcomes against the initial goals.
