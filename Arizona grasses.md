# Arizona grasses

## Core idea
Blue Grama (Bouteloua gracilis) — the classic high-desert grassland grass, drought-tolerant, good forage, stabilizes soil
Sideoats Grama (Bouteloua curtipendula) — Arizona's unofficial state grass, distinctive oat-like seed heads, good for erosion control and forage
Sand Dropseed (Sporobolus cryptandrus) — very drought tolerant, common on disturbed ground
Muhlenbergia species (various muhly grasses) — ornamental and functional, several native to Arizona uplands
Galleta grass (Hilaria jamesii) — tough, drought-hardy, good for erosion control on rangeland
