# B Vitamins

## Core idea
B vitamins are a group of eight distinct, water-soluble nutrients that act as the building blocks for a healthy body. Unlike fat-soluble vitamins, your body cannot store B vitamins (except for B12)

• B1 (Thiamin): Breaks down carbohydrates to generate energy. Vital for brain and nerve function.
• B2 (Riboflavin): Supports energy production, clear vision, and healthy skin. 
• B3 (Niacin): Helps manage cholesterol, aids digestion, and keeps the nervous system functioning properly.
• B5 (Pantothenic Acid): Crucial for manufacturing hormones, lipids, and hemoglobin.
• B6 (Pyridoxine): Involved in immune system health, brain development, and the production of mood-regulating neurotransmitters (serotonin and dopamine).
• B7 (Biotin): Best known for supporting keratin production to maintain hair, skin, and nail health.
• B9 (Folate / Folic Acid): Crucial for DNA synthesis and cell division. 
• B12 (Cobalamin): Essential for maintaining a healthy nervous system and producing red blood cells.

• Whole Grains & Legumes (High in B1, B2, B5): Oats, brown rice, barley, lentils, chickpeas, and black beans.
• Leafy Greens (High in B9/Folate): Spinach, kale, romaine lettuce, and asparagus.
• Fortified Foods & Yeast: Nutritional yeast (an exceptional vegan source of B12) and fortified breakfast cereals.
