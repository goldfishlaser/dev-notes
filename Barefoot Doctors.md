# Barefoot Doctors

A practical guide for community health workers published in 1960s-70s China that significantly improved rural health in China. 

https://archive.org/details/barefootdoctorsm0000huna

Improving community health by reducing reliance on overly centralized and capitalistic healthcare system.

More information on success of the program:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6124148/
