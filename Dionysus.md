# Dionysus

## Core idea
Dionysus is a god of fruits and vegetation, [[wine]] and ecstasy. Dionysus has a special place in my pantheon, representing a holiness which is possible through sensitivity and bonding created via revelrous experiences. Revelry is sometimes stigmatised by some straight-edge ecologists as "excess" - whereas I think there are times of excess and times of restraint, and while it may not be the easiest road or a road without dangers - I do not regret the moments I spend in revelry and I stand up for our right to party. :)

* the caravaggio painting of Dionysus is one of my favorites
