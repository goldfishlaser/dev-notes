# E600 glue

## Core idea
. Tetrachloroethylene (50% – 75%)
. Styrene-Butadiene Copolymer (10% – 25%)

dries clear and stays elastic
withstands extreme temperatures (from -40°F to 180°F)
for 
• Plastics (except (see below))
• Metal, wood, glass, and fiberglass
• Ceramics, masonry, and concrete
• Leather, rubber, vinyl, and fabric

not recommended for use on polystyrene, polyethylene, polypropylene, Styrofoam, or paper products
