# GTD

## Core idea
Getting Things Done 

Capture - Record all pending tasks to an external system
Clarify - Is it actionable? 
Organize - 
  * Next Actions — the very next physical step, often split by context (@calls, @computer, @errands)
  * Projects — anything requiring more than one action step to complete
  * Waiting For — things delegated or blocked on someone else
  * Someday/Maybe — not actionable now, but not dead either
  * Calendar — only for things tied to a specific date/time, not a wishlist of "should do"
Reflect 
 Go through every list, close what's done, capture what slipped through, re-clarify anything vague.
Engage 

if a clarified action takes under two minutes, just do it immediately rather than tracking it.
