# Ginkgo Berry Alternative

## Core idea
Ginkgo Berry is used in [[JADAM]] because it is toxic to the pests and native to the region. I am in Southwest USA, so I should investigate the appropriate plant from my area. 

- Datura
- 


• The Ratio: Fill a large pot roughly half full with your chosen native plant material (chopped roots, leaves, or pods), and fill the rest with soft water (distilled or clean rainwater).
• The Boil: Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 5 hours to fully extract the chemical defenses.
• Storage: Strain the liquid into heat-resistant bottles while still boiling hot, leaving no air pockets, and seal tightly for an indefinite shelf life.
