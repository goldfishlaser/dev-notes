# Iron

## Core idea

• Adult Men: 15 mg daily.
• Adult Women (Ages 19–50): 33 mg daily. 

The requirement is 1.8 times higher for plant based men and women


• white beans 
• lentils
• spinach
• tofu
• pumpkin seeds
• fortified breakfast cereals.
