# JADAM Sulfur (JS)

## Core idea
Fungicide and germicide 

For 100 L - 25kg [[Sulfur]], 20kg Caustic Soda [[sodium hydroxide]], 1.5kg Sea Salt, 1 kg Fine Soil, 82L water

safety: Always add the hydroxide to water, never water to the hydroxide
