# Johnny Decimal

## Core idea
Give every category and every "thing" inside it a fixed, memorable number

Everything sits inside a strict three-level hierarchy:

- Areas (10-19, 20-29, 30-39...), broad domains, max 10 areas
- Categories (11, 12, 13... within area 10-19), max 10 categories per area
- IDs (11.01, 11.02, 11.03...), max 100 individual items per category
