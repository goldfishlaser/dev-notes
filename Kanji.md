# Kanji

## Core idea
Kanji are logographic Chinese characters, historically adapted from Chinese writing scripts, used in the writing of Japanese.

Kanji characters are built out of smaller constituent parts called components and radicals. Radicals and components appear on the left (hen), bottom (ashi), enclosing the character (kamae), or hanging down (tare).
