# M-C-M

## Core idea
M-C-M' (Money-Commodity-Money prime) is the general formula for capital in Karl Marx’s theory, describing the process where money is invested to purchase commodities, which are then sold for a greater amount of money ($M' > M$)

The increase in value from $M$ to $M'$ is generated through the exploitation of labor power, a unique commodity whose use in production creates more value than it costs to reproduce.
