# Magnesium

## Core idea

The recommended daily allowance (RDA) for adults is 400–420 mg for men and 310–320 mg for women.

• Magnesium Glycinate: Highly bioavailable and gentle on the stomach. Best for anxiety, stress, and improving sleep.
• Magnesium Citrate: Well-absorbed but pulls water into the intestines. Commonly used to treat constipation.
• Magnesium Malate: Readily absorbed and bound to malic acid. Excellent for fatigue and muscle soreness.
• Magnesium Oxide: Poorly absorbed by the body. Frequently found in cheaper supplements but mostly used to relieve acid reflux or indigestion.

• Seeds & Nuts: Pumpkin seeds (156 mg per ounce), chia seeds (111 mg per ounce), and almonds (80 mg per ounce).
• Spinach: Cooked spinach (157 mg per cup) is exceptionally rich.
• Whole Grains: Boiled quinoa (118 mg per cup) and brown rice.
• Legumes: Black beans (120 mg per cup) and edamame.
• Dark Chocolate: 64 mg per 1-ounce square (look for 70–85% cacao).
