# PAPRA

## Core idea
I volunteer with tetra bio distributed to share information about their open source powered air respirator project (PAPRA) that provides N100 level filtration protection in scenarios where the user may require protection for extended time. It has a parts bom that is under 200 bucks and completely available off the shelf. 
This technology is used in OET whenever we need protection from airborne contaminants.
