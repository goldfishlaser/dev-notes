# PVC repair

## Core idea

For the repair PVC piping:
* to connect PVC slip and slip parts together requires a pvc specific cement and primer for plumbing applications. Apply the wand of material around and then while placing make about a 45 degree twist in one direction in order to spread and activate the sealing process. Note: threaded PVC components also exist
* to cut PVC parts, you need either a pipe cutter or saw. If you're using a saw though, this can create a lot of plastic shavings which are very important not to let back into your plumbing system. 
* our well system repair introduced me to the use of elbows, couplings, a union, reducers and male adapters. 
* PVC pipe is usually marked with its dimension in inches, but if not measure the outside diameter to determine size.
* Most home applications will use schedule 20 pvc, but if you need high temperature or pressure schedule 40
