# Potassium

Potassium is an essential mineral and vital electrolyte.

The dietary guideline for adequate daily intake is 3,400 mg for adult men and 2,600 mg for adult women.

• Beet Greens: 1,300 mg per cooked cup.
• White Beans & Lima Beans: Around 950–1,000 mg per cooked cup.
• Baked Potatoes & Sweet Potatoes: 900–950 mg for one medium potato (with the skin on).
• Avocados: 690 mg per medium avocado.
• Spinach: 840 mg per cooked cup.
• Bananas: 422 mg per medium fruit.
