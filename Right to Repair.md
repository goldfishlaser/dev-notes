# Right to Repair

## What this is
Right to repair is a legal right for owners of devices and equipment to freely maintain, repair, or modify products
If consumers don't demand and exercise this right, they will become increasingly affected by supply chain problems, waste, and rent-seeking behaviors by businesses.
