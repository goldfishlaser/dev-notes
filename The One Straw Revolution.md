# The One Straw Revolution

He argues for a natural farming, advocates for no cultivation, no chemical fertilizers, and no weeding. Fukuoka argues that human intervention often damages the earth, and true agricultural and personal harmony comes from working with nature rather than trying to conquer it.

## Key claims I'm thinking with
- Avoid plowing
- Avoid chemical fertilizers and prefer ground cover to compost
- Control weeds by competition, groundcover, and mulch

* In my opinion, overavoidance of plowing when reviving compacted soil is sometimes not wise. Certain weeds are easier to compete with than others. Important to know what you're dealing with. But will test some claims.
