# Vitamin D

## Core idea
• Vitamin D3 (Cholecalciferol): This is the form produced naturally by your skin from sunlight 

• Vitamin D2 (Ergocalciferol): This form is derived from plants, mushrooms exposed to UV light, and fortified foods.

The primary way to consume plant-based D3 is through supplements that harvest Lichen (a unique symbiotic organism consisting of fungi and algae) or specific microalgae species.

Spending 5 to 30 minutes in direct sunlight twice a week (without sunscreen on a portion of your skin, like your arms or legs) allows your body to manufacture its own D3.

If you are not getting enough sun exposure, the baseline recommended daily amount to prevent deficiency is 600 to 800 International Units (IU).

People with obesity often require 2 to 3 times more vitamin D to reach optimal blood levels.
