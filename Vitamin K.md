# Vitamin K

## Core idea
• Vitamin K1 (Phylloquinone): Found mainly in green leafy plants and brassicas.
• Vitamin K2 (Menaquinone): Found in fermented foods (like [[natto]]). Bacteria in your gut also make it.

The recommended adequate intake (AI) is 120 micrograms (mcg) daily for men and 90 mcg daily for women.

Your body absorbs it much better when it is consumed alongside dietary fats.

Related to blood and bone health.
