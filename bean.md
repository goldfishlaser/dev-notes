# bean

## Core idea
Beans are one of the most important crops on the homestead. They provide many of the essential nutrients and protein required by the diet. 

OET will focus on the production of:
Soybean
Black bean
Lentil
Garbanzo bean
Pole bean
Bush bean
Sweet pea
