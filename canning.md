# canning

## Core idea

- Canning is an efficient way to preserve food, requiring a couple of hours

- Canned goods are shelf-stable for 1–5+ years at room temperature

- Canning safety comes down to following tested time/temperature/acidity parameters

There are multiple canning methods and its important to pick the right one for the job:

[[steam canning]] - For high acid foods only
[[pressure canning]] - Required for low acid food
