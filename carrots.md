# carrots

## Core idea

* Plant in early August
* Carrots need loose soil free of rocks, clumps, and hard clay; till or loosen soil to a depth of 10 to 12 inches.
* Mix in plenty of organic matter like compost or coconut coir to improve drainage. Do not use heavy nitrogen fertilizer 
* 1/4 inch deep and space them about 1 inch apart.
* Cover the damp seedbed with cardboard. Check daily and remove the cover the exact moment you see green sprouts.
*  Once seedlings are 2 inches tall, snip them with scissors so they are spaced 3 to 4 inches apart.
*  Provide 1 inch of water per week. Soak the soil deeply rather than watering shallowly every day. This encourages the roots to grow deep into the ground.
*  Apply a thin layer of mulch around the green tops once they are established to keep the soil cool and retain moisture.
*  Chilly late-autumn temperatures trigger carrots to convert their starches into sugars
*  fertilize with [[phosphorous]].
