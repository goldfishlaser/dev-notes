# chaos magick

## Core idea
Chaos magick is a meta-occult system that uses magical, religious, and spiritual beliefs as pragmatic tools. 

While I find empirical rationality to be the most concrete epistemology for truth-determining, I find power from practcies related to the deities ganesh, sante de muerte, and global gods of agriculture and wisdom. My biological lineage is celtic and anglosaxon, but I feel an affinity for practices by people all over the world. So far, my favorite religion is Taoism, but I think there are interesting things to learn from all religions. I enjoy tarot and other stochastic practices using archetypal imagery. I am just a little hesitant to mess with any monotheistic religions, even though I have a lot of knowledge of Christianity from my past.
