# closet organization

## Core idea
The proper storage of textiles prevents damage and assists in their use.

Here are some ideas:
* 3D printed rod mount
* Covering cardboard boxes shoe boxes with fabric 
* Covering small flat pieces of cardboard with fabric as section organizers
* Command hooks (or 3D printed hooks) for hanging hats, etc
* Tension rods as boot racks
* Freestanding closet frames
  
  * to prevent pests, seal the cardboard and thoroughly glue edge to edge.
