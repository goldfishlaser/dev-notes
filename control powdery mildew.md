# control powdery mildew

## Core idea
Fungicide

mixing [[JADAM Sulfur (JS)]] and [[JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA)]] with soft water.

Mixing and Dosing
• Per Gallon Ratio: Mix 1 teaspoon of JADAM Sulfur (JS) and 2 teaspoons of JADAM Wetting Agent (JWA) per gallon of water.
• Smaller Sprayer (2-Liter): Use half a teaspoon of JS and 1 teaspoon of JWA.
• Water Quality: Use soft water like distilled water, reverse osmosis water, or clean rainwater so the soap (JWA) does not fail.

Application Tips
• Fine Mist: Spray a fine mist to cover both the top and bottom surfaces of every leaf.
• Timing: Spray only in the evening or on an overcast day with no direct sunlight to prevent leaf burn.
• Targeted Use: Spray only when fungal signs first appear rather than treating the whole garden proactively.
