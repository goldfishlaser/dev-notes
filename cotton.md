# cotton

## Core idea
- Gossypium thurberi, a wild cotton plant; Upland (Gossypium hirsutum) and Pima (Gossypium barbadense) are grown in AZ
- Cotton loves hot weather, but does require water
- start seeds indoors 4 to 8 weeks before the last frost and transplant them carefully, plant seedlings in full sun and well-draining soil after the last spring frost when soil temperatures reach 60°F - although you also have to follow laws about when you can plant.
  - 1 inch deep. Space them 18 to 30 inches apart in rows or groups of three seeds every 6 inches.
- Water moderately every 7 days
- fertilize when flowering

Important: There are a lot of laws about growing cotton, you cant just do it willy nilly...
- You cannot leave cotton plants standing year-round. After harvesting, you are legally required to shred all crop remnants and till the land to completely destroy the host plant and its root system so no stalks remain attached to the soil.
- You can only by certified seeds
- Cannot grow commercially
