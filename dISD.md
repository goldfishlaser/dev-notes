# dISD

## Core idea
dISD is a work in progress, aimed to model instructional design (ISD) elements in a DOF compatible way. Instructional Design by its nature is already well suited for distributive instruction because it was invented by the military, but dISD will just take it a step farther by providing schemas and ontologies.
