# duree

## Core idea
Henri Bergson introduced the concept of la durée (duration) in Time and Free Will to challenge the mechanistic, scientific view of time as a series of discrete, measurable units, "clock time".  He argued that real time is a qualitative, continuous, and indivisible flow of lived experience.  Clock time is a spatialized abstraction that distorts reality.
