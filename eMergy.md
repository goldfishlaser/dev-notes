# eMergy

## Core idea
 eMergy: "embodied energy" the total available energy (usually solar equivalent) that was used, directly or indirectly, to make a product or service. 

Transformity: emergy input per unit of output (sej/unit). Higher transformity = more energy "concentrated" to produce that unit

Empower: The flow rate of emergy through a system per unit of time (sej/time).

Ecological economics and systems ecology concepts introduced by H.T. Odum to measure the real cost and quality of energy.
