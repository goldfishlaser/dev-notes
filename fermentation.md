# Fermentation

## What this is
Fermentation is an anaerobic metabolic process in which microorganisms like yeast and bacteria break down carbohydrates (such as sugars and starches) into simpler substances like alcohols, organic acids, and gases to release energy. 

The bacterias are often good for the gut. It also extends the lifespan of produce.
