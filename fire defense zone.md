# fire defense zone

## Core idea

The fire defense zone must be free of combustable materials. Usually, it is prescribed to be 5 feet around the entire house. I am choosing to enforce it in the sides of the house which are most likely to be facing the direction of a wildfire. 

It is also important to clear around the area of the shed, because if the shed catches fire it is a hazard to the house. 

Flammable plants:
- Juniper
- Cypress
- Ornamental grasses
