# florist wire

## Core idea
Usually made of aluminum, iron, or stainless steel

• Lower numbers (e.g., 16-gauge or 18-gauge) mean thicker, stiffer wire used for heavy flower heads like sunflowers, large branches, or wreath building.
• Higher numbers (e.g., 28-gauge to 32-gauge) mean thinner, more flexible wire used for delicate petals, corsages, boutonnieres, and floral crowns.
