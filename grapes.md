# grapes

## Core idea
Ideal time to plant: Late Fall
Sun Requirements: Full Sun
Watering Requirements: Give vines roughly 1 inch of water (about 5 gallons over a 3-by-3-foot area) every week for first two years or after during a deep drought situation
Maintenance: Pinch off all the flowers and fruit the first year so that it focuses on its roots. Mulch a few inches from the trunk, 4 inches deep and 2 feet around
Location: On arch trellises near locations where the sun is heating the house or zone 0-1 areas.
