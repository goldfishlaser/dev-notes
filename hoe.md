# hoe

## Core idea
Blade of carbon, hardened or stainless steel securely attached by a tight eye, tang, weld, or ferrule to wooden handle (30-40mm, 1.5 kg). The blade must be angled to easily loosen dirt and be sharp enough for shallow weeding, usually in between 4-8 inches wide.
