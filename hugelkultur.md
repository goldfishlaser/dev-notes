# hugelkultur

## Core idea
Building raised garden beds by layering large logs and branches at the base, covering them with organic waste, and topping them with soil. 

In my high desert climate it is recommended that I also dig a trench a about 1-3 feet to retain more water.

I mostly use whatever wood and grass clippings etc that I can source from neighbors and our compost.
