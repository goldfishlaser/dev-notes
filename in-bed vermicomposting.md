# in-bed vermicomposting

## Core idea
In-bed vermicomposting solves the problem of what to do with the worms during extremes of hot and cold. 

The best time to add in-bed vermicomposting is from mid-September through mid-April. Local red wigglers are the best to introduce.

Worms like watermelon, bananas, sweet potatoes, pumpkin, strawberries, apples, beans, pears, carrots, cherries, grapes, peaches, spinach, cucumbers, cabbage, celery, mangoes, tomatoes, corn, cardboard, shredded paper
