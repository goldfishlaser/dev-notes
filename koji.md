# koji

## Core idea
Aspergillus oryzae
grown on cooked grains or beans
Used for [[sake]], [[miso]], [[soy sauce]], and[[shio koji]]
a standard culinary batch of koji rice is harvested after 48 hours

Koji spores, known in Japanese as tane-koji (seed koji) or koji-kin, are produced by letting a batch of koji complete its entire reproductive life cycle.

 keeping a pure strain of Aspergillus oryzae alive without it mutating or getting taken over by toxic wild molds is difficult, but if trying, adding hardwood ash is an important step
