# microfactory

## Core idea

Microfactories are compact, highly automated production facilities designed for flexible, localized, and small-batch manufacturing. 

Our microfactory will produce:
* gutters for water capture
* replacement plastic parts
* open hardware projects such as Recreator3Ds, etc
* custom plastic enclosures
* basic handtools
* mills
* self 
* hdpe 2x4s
* metal machining
* ceramics
* etc
