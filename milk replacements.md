# milk replacements

## Core idea
buttermilk - per 1 cup [[plant milk]] add 1 tablespoon lemon juice or vinegar and wait 10 minutes

condensed milk - 4 oz silken tofu, 1/4 cup soymilk, 1/4 cup sugar or maple syrup
