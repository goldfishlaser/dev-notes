# nutrient combos and conflicts

## Core idea

Nutrients that conflict with each other:
- [[Calcium]] and [[Iron]]
- [[Zinc]] and Copper
- Zinc oand Manganese or Iron
- Zinc and Calcium (Lesser)
- Tannins and Iron
1-2 hours is usually sufficient as a buffer
  
Nutrients that boost each other:
Fat soluble vitamins are A, D, E, K, CoQ10 therefore, take these with high fat meals or fat supplements.
- [[Vitamin D]] and Calcium
- C and Iron
- [[Vitamin K]] and D 
- [[Magnesium]] and D

Calcium, magnesium, vitamin C, [[Potassium]], [[B Vitamins]], and Zinc are good to focus on multiple times a day. 

Iron is good to take on an empty stomach.
