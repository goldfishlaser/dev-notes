# papercrete

## Core idea
3 parts wet paper pulp (from recycled newspaper, junk mail, or cardboard soaked in water for 24–48 hours and blended)
2 parts [[Portland cement]]
1 part coarse sand
Water as needed to achieve an oatmeal-like consistency
