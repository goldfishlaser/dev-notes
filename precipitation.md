# precipitation

## Core idea
Precipitation is measured as the vertical depth of water that would accumulate on a horizontal surface if none were lost to evaporation, runoff, or infiltration. 

Measured by rain gauge (pluviometer), a funnel and graduated cylinder.

NWS uses 20-inch gauge and 8-inch diamter funnel.
