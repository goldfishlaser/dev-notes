# recreator3d

## Core idea
Recreator3D pultruders turn PET bottles into filament.

- To remove ridges from bottles, its possible to use a Ryobi Power Inflator and expand until gauge reading 20. We prefer to use smooth bottles like Smart Water comes in. 
- The community offers a bottle cutter which can be used to prepare the bottle for the pultruder
