# santa muerte

## Core idea
I first encountered the image of Santa Muerte on a candle at a store. I immediately fell in love, purchased the candle and researched her meaning and how its recommended to interact with her. Santa Muerte is a saint for the disposssessed, outcasts, and outlaws, and I love her for that. She is also someone to confide in about romance, money, power, and death. I pay special attention to her alter in the autumn to prepare for day of the dead.
