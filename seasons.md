# seasons

## Core idea
People often think of the four seasons Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter. The conditions are created by the Earth's axial tilt, its annual revolution around the Sun, and axial parallelism.

Some cultures also offer the concept of microseasons, such as japan and china.  These consist of twenty-four solar terms, each split into three five-day pentads.
