# seed saving

## Core idea
Saving seeds is a critical activity of food production.

The basic principles of seed saving include choosing open-pollinated or heirloom varieties, selecting seeds from the healthiest plants, and ensuring seeds are fully mature, clean, and stored in dark, dry, cool conditions. Always be sure to label with plant name, variety, and harvest date.
