# shade structures

## Core idea
A building or structure that creates shade. 

The morning (east sun) results in shade structure adding shadow to the west.

The midday (high sun) results in shade under or very close to the structure. This shadow is longer in winter.

The late afternoon (west sun) results in shade toward the east.
