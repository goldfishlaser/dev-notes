# six sigma

## Core idea
a data-driven business method used to improve quality, reduce errors, and cut waste. Its core aims include eliminating defects, improving work processes, and focusing on the customer.

DMAIC: Used to improve existing products or processes by defining, measuring, analyzing, improving, and controlling them.

DMADV: Used to create new products or processes from scratch by defining, measuring, analyzing, designing, and verifying them
