# three bin dishwashing

## Core idea
The three bins are for washing, rinsing, and sanitizing.

Before you begin: scrape as best one can all the food off of the plates
In Bin 1, fill with warm water (at least 110°F) and dish soap or manual detergent. Scrub items thoroughly with a sponge or brush.
In Bin 2, fill with clean, warm water. Dip and move the scrubbed dishes here to wash away all leftover soap and loose particles.
In Bin 3, fill with water and mix in a chemical sanitizer. Soak dishes for requisite time.
