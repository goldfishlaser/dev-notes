# wfpb

## Core idea
Whole Foods Plant-Based is a dietary practice of consuming plants closer to their natural forms, instead of in processed ways such as oil, etc. This way of eating could be beneficial due to the fact that oil production requires a lot of seeds and processing equipment and therefore likely a luxury product on the homestead.
