# wine

## Core idea
Fruit + yeast and time makes wine

• 3 cans (12 oz each) Welch’s 100% frozen grape juice concentrate
• 2.5 cups granulated white sugar 
• 1 packet (about 1 tsp) Red Star Premier Cuvée
• Water (to top off to the 1-gallon mark)

Put it in a jug with an airlock (or burp container manually)

Wait 2 to 3 weeks

Siphon off the sludge or just let it settle /shrug
