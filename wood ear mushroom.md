# wood ear mushroom

## Core idea
100g of dried wood ear, it contains 10.6g of protein, 0.2g of fat, 65g of carbohydrates, 7g of fiber, 5.8g of ash, 375mg of calcium, 201mg of phosphorus, 185mg of iron, and 0.03mg of carotene, along with 21 free amino acids, ergosterol, 6 fatty acids, vitamins B1, B2, D, and niacin
