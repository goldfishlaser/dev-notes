# xicalicoliuhqui motif

## Core idea
Xicalcoliuhqui (also referred to as a "step fret" or "stepped fret" design and greca in Spanish) is a common motif in Mesoamerican art. It is composed of three or more steps connected to a hook or spiral, reminiscent of a "greek-key" meander.

Found in the Codex Magliabechiano, a pictorial Aztec codex created during the mid-16th century, in the early Spanish colonial period.
